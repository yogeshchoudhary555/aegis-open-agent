AEGIS Open Agent. A business agent that answers from your real data — and shows its receipts.

Business data lives in fragments. Customers in a C-R-M. Orders in a store. Tickets in support. Most chatbots guess across that mess. This agent does not guess.

Ask it a plain question. What is the total revenue? It plans a query, runs it through the federation layer, and answers: twenty-five thousand and eighty-three — with the exact query and the source cited.

Which product sells the most by revenue? Mechanical Keyboard, at seven thousand four hundred and ninety-seven. Again, query shown, source shown.

How many support tickets are open? Three. Every answer carries an explain-why trace: the plan, the queries, the sources, the lane, and the latency.

The data layer follows the Zetaris pattern: one query layer over many sources, without moving the data. The power layer follows the Meterless pattern: the right model in each lane — fast for aggregates, reasoning for ranked answers.

And it only reads. There is no write path at all — only a single select query is ever executed. So it cannot delete, order, or message anything by design.

Try it yourself. The repo, the live deployment, and the setup instructions are in the description. Ask it: what is the total revenue?
